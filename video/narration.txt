TaalTrainer is a tabla practice studio that runs in your browser with zero audio samples and zero network requests. Every stroke is synthesized live using three Web Audio worklet processors. Ringing right hand strokes sum five damped sine modes near a harmonic series, while the bass hand adds a pitch glide and dry finger taps shape short noise bursts. All thirteen bol strokes sit on the home row, and seven synthesis sliders let you adjust tuning, ring decay, slap transient, and hand levels in real time. Below the keyboard, the taal player loops Teentaal, Keherwa, Dadra, and Ektaal from forty to two hundred forty beats per minute with beat lamps, sam markers, and live Devanagari theka tracking. In the learner path, twelve progressive lessons teach through call and response, grading your timing and stroke accuracy within a tempo scaled millisecond window. Finally, the notation recorder captures your playing, filters mechanical key bounce, and prints Devanagari bol notation on a beat grid that you can replay at any time.
